Privacy information.

Data minimization

What we store

Account profile, conversations, health-state information, assessment reports, timelines, follow-ups, emergency events and doctor handoffs may be stored to provide requested features.

Purpose

Why it is used

Data is used to provide personalized health assistance, maintain continuity, generate reports, support follow-ups and deliver requested emergency workflows.

Access

Who can access it

Users access their own data. Protected administrative features are restricted by role. Doctor handoff sharing is controlled separately through the Security Center.

Controls

Manage your data

Account security, memory, consent, export and deletion controls are intentionally located in the dedicated Security Center.
